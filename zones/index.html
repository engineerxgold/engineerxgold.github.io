How the zones are made

  1. Daily, 4H and 1H charts are scanned on closed broker candles only. 30m and 15m show swing highs and lows.
  2. A zone needs a real reason: an order block, a fair value gap, a liquidity grab or a Wyckoff/VSA test.
  3. Each zone gets a grade (A, B or C) from how many confirmations line up: trend, premium/discount, location and volume.
  4. Every zone has one cancel level. A candle close beyond it removes the zone.
  5. Entries come later, only after a 5m and 1m confirmation inside the zone.

Volume is broker tick volume, not exchange volume.
