Liquidity Runs and Market Mechanics

Price movement in the precious metals market is heavily driven by liquidity pools. Swing highs, swing lows, as well as previous day and previous week extremes, act as magnets where stop-losses and breakout orders accumulate. Institutional order flow frequently targets these areas to fill large institutional volume before executing a reversal or continuation.

Recognizing where liquidity rests helps traders avoid entering premature positions inside random noise. Instead of chasing momentum, patient market participants wait for price to interact with structured zones after a liquidity sweep has occurred. This confluence provides a clearer risk-to-reward framework.
