Understanding Liquidity Sweeps in Gold

Zone identification alone is only half the battle; understanding how price interacts with liquidity is vital for accurate market timing. In our framework, swing highs, swing lows, as well as previous day and previous week highs and lows, act as primary liquidity pools where institutional orders often rest.

Price frequently migrates toward these liquidity levels to trigger stop-losses before reacting off our predetermined technical zones. By mapping these liquidity pools alongside our backtested zones, traders gain a comprehensive view of market mechanics without relying on empty promises or standard signals.
