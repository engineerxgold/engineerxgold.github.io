The Role of Liquidity in Gold Trading

Price movement is heavily driven by liquidity pools resting above swing highs and below swing lows, as well as previous day and week extremes. The xauusd price frequently gravitates toward these areas to trigger resting stop-loss orders before making impulsive directional moves.

Recognizing where liquidity accumulates prevents traders from entering positions prematurely. Instead of chasing momentum, patient market participants wait for price to interact with established zones and liquidity sweeps.
