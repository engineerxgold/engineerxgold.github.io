What is a precision zone?

A point of interest from a trading book (OTE 0.705, balanced price range, weekly close level, daily order block) that held better than random levels on 10 years of gold. Each zone has an invalidation price.

How often are the zones updated?

A daily plan at the Asian open, then after the Asian, London and New York sessions.

Are these trade signals?

No. Zones are areas to watch. Wait for your own confirmation and manage risk.
