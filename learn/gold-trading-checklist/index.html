Gold Trading Entry Checklist: HTF Zone, Confirmation and Risk

A free, practical checklist for XAUUSD traders. Save or print it before your next session.

1. Check the data

Use a current broker quote and completed candles. A missing or stale feed means wait.

2. Check the calendar

Read the USD release schedule. New trade ideas are blocked around protected news windows.

3. Find the location

Mark a qualifying D1/H4/H1 zone or level, or a confirmed M30/M15 swing. Record its invalidation and wait for an entry model.

4. Wait for the first test

Price must actually reach the zone. An alert explains where to watch; it is not an entry order.

5. Confirm the M5 move

Look for a completed directional structure break and displacement that create a fair value gap.

6. Observe the M5 retest

Wait for price to return to that new gap. An old historical touch cannot substitute for a live observed retest.

7. Confirm the M1 entry

After the retest, wait for a completed M1 structure break and new gap in the same direction.

8. Write entry and stop

Choose the defined pullback price. Place the planned price stop beyond the post-retest swing with a spread buffer.

9. Check the target

Use an actual structural reference. Reject a nearby obstacle or a target that does not meet the required reward-to-risk.

10. Size the risk

Calculate size from your account risk budget, broker contract specification and stop distance. Include costs.

11. Respect the expiry

A missed limit entry stays missed. Cancel a pending plan after expiry, news protection, invalidation or loss of verified data.

12. Keep an honest journal

Record losses, cancellations and uncertain outcomes as well as targets. Automated quote observations are not your actual account results.

Tick volume is a broker activity proxy. Weekly COT and delayed macro observations provide context; they do not guarantee the next move.
